Helping You Find the Root Cause and Restore Lasting Health

I believe everyone deserves to feel healthy, energized, and able to enjoy life. Whether you're struggling with digestive concerns, hormone imbalances, chronic illness, skin conditions, fatigue, or simply don't feel like yourself, my goal is to uncover why your symptoms are occurring and help restore balance from the inside out.

As a Naturopathic Doctor, I combine evidence-based medicine with holistic, individualized care to identify the underlying causes of illness—not simply manage symptoms. I take the time to truly listen, understand your story, and develop personalized treatment plans that support long-term health, vitality, and disease prevention.

My educational background includes a Bachelor of Arts, Master of Arts in Kinesiology with a focus on healthy aging and older adults, designation as a Registered Holistic Nutritionist (RHN), and a Doctor of Naturopathy (ND). This diverse training allows me to integrate medical science with nutrition, movement, and lifestyle medicine to provide comprehensive, patient-centered care.

Philosophy

My Approach

Healing is rarely about one symptom or one diagnosis. I believe the body functions as an interconnected system where physical health, nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, emotional well-being, relationships, and lifestyle all influence healing.

My role is to bring these pieces together, identify the underlying causes of imbalance, and develop a personalized treatment plan that supports your body's remarkable ability to heal.

Treatment plans may include:

  • Advanced blood work and functional laboratory testing
  • Clinical nutrition and personalized dietary guidance
  • Botanical medicine and targeted supplementation
  • Acupuncture and Traditional Chinese Medicine
  • Lifestyle, sleep, and stress management
  • Facial acupuncture and natural skin rejuvenation

Continuing Education

A Commitment to Lifelong Learning

I regularly attend postgraduate conferences and advanced clinical training to remain current with the latest research in functional and integrative medicine. My ongoing education includes advanced hormone prescribing, functional laboratory interpretation, and Compassionate Inquiry®, developed by Dr. Gabor Maté.

I have also completed advanced certification in Quantum Reflex Analysis (QRA) and incorporate advanced laboratory testing through leading functional laboratories worldwide. My commitment is to continually integrate the best available scientific evidence with the wisdom of traditional naturopathic medicine.

My Promise to My Patients

I believe healing begins when patients feel truly heard. My commitment is to provide compassionate, thorough, and evidence-informed care while empowering you with the knowledge, tools, and support to take an active role in your health.

It would be my privilege to support you on your journey toward lasting health.
