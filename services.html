What I Offer

Services & Modalities

Integrative, evidence-based approaches to naturopathic medicine, tailored to your unique physiological needs.

Clinical Tools

Therapies & Treatments

Carefully selected therapies to support your healing journey, applied individually or in combination as your health requires.

Botanical Medicine

Therapeutic use of plant-based medicines — tinctures, teas, capsules, and topical preparations — to support the body's natural healing processes. Plant medicines are selected based on evidence and individual need.

Blood & Functional Lab Testing

Comprehensive blood panels and advanced functional laboratory testing — including DUTCH hormone testing, GI mapping, micronutrient analysis, and more — to identify the physiological root causes of your symptoms.

Clinical Nutrition & Dietary Guidance

Evidence-informed nutritional strategies tailored to your unique biology, health goals, and lifestyle. Includes meal planning, therapeutic diets, targeted supplementation, and practical guidance you can actually sustain.

Traditional Chinese Medicine & Acupuncture

Acupuncture, auricular therapy, cupping, and moxibustion to support pain, stress, hormonal balance, digestive health, and more. Rooted in centuries of practice and supported by modern research.

Facial Acupuncture & Skin Rejuvenation

A natural, non-invasive approach to healthy skin aging. Facial acupuncture supports collagen production, reduces fine lines, improves skin tone, and addresses underlying imbalances contributing to skin concerns.

Lifestyle & Stress Medicine

Sleep optimization, stress resilience, movement strategies, and mindfulness-informed approaches — integrating Compassionate Inquiry® to support the connection between emotional well-being and physical health.

Homeopathy & Biological Medicine

Classical homeopathy alongside modern biological medicine including Pleo Sanum, Regeno Therapy®, and gemmotherapy — to support gentle yet effective whole-body healing at the cellular level.

Quantum Reflex Analysis (QRA®)

An advanced assessment method that complements standard clinical evaluation. QRA® provides additional insight into organ system function, nutritional status, and environmental stressors to further individualize your treatment plan.
