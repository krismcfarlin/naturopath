Frequently Asked Questions

Please ensure you have completed the online intake forms sent via JaneApp at least 24 hours prior to your appointment. Bring any recent lab work or medical records that may be relevant to your health concerns.

Naturopathic services are typically not covered by OHIP, but they are covered by most extended health benefit plans. We do not offer direct billing, but will provide you with a detailed invoice to submit to your provider for reimbursement.

We require 48 hours notice for appointment cancellations or rescheduling. Appointments changed or cancelled with less than 48 hours notice will be subject to a cancellation fee equal to the cost of the visit.
