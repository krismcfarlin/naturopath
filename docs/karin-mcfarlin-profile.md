## Biography

I believe everyone deserves to feel healthy, energized, and able to enjoy life. Whether you're struggling with digestive concerns, hormone imbalances, chronic illness, skin conditions, fatigue, or simply don't feel like yourself, my goal is to uncover **why** your symptoms are occurring and help restore balance from the inside out.

As a Naturopathic Doctor, I combine evidence-based medicine with holistic, individualized care to identify the underlying causes of illness—not simply manage symptoms. I take the time to truly listen, understand your story, and develop personalized treatment plans that support long-term health, vitality, and disease prevention.

My educational background includes a **Bachelor of Arts**, **Master of Arts in Kinesiology** with a focus on healthy aging and older adults, designation as a **Registered Holistic Nutritionist (RHN)**, and a **Doctor of Naturopathy (ND)**. This diverse training allows me to integrate medical science with nutrition, movement, and lifestyle medicine to provide comprehensive, patient-centered care.

## My Approach

Healing is rarely about one symptom or one diagnosis.

I believe the body functions as an interconnected system where physical health, nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, emotional well-being, relationships, and lifestyle all influence healing. My role is to bring these pieces together, identify the underlying causes of imbalance, and develop a personalized treatment plan that supports your body's remarkable ability to heal.

**Treatment plans may include:**
- Advanced blood work and functional laboratory testing
- Clinical nutrition and personalized dietary guidance
- Botanical medicine and targeted supplementation
- Acupuncture and Traditional Chinese Medicine
- Lifestyle, sleep, and stress management
- Facial acupuncture and natural skin rejuvenation
- Advanced assessment techniques to further individualize treatment
